Everything between a watched video and a song on your stick

ck-you3 does one job: it gets the music you already found on YouTube onto the drive you actually listen from.

  • Import your watch history

    Bring in the watch-history file from Google Takeout. Import again later and new videos are merged in, never duplicated.

  • Find any song in seconds

    Search thousands of watched videos by title or channel, and filter to what is not downloaded yet or already on your drive.

  • Real MP3 files

    Each track is extracted with yt-dlp and encoded to MP3 by ffmpeg — plain files that play in a car, a speaker or any music player.

  • Straight to your USB drive

    Plug in a USB stick and the app offers it as a destination. Or pick any folder on your computer instead.

  • Never the same song twice

    Songs already on the destination are recognised and skipped, so topping up a drive only adds what is new.

  • Catches what you just watched

    Optionally, the app reads your browser's history in Chrome, Brave, Edge or Chromium and shows the videos you just played. Off until you turn it on.

How it works

Three steps, and the second time it is only the last one.

  1. Export your history

    Request your YouTube history from Google Takeout in JSON format, then import the watch-history file into ck-you3.

  2. Pick your songs

    Search, filter and tick the tracks you want. The ones already on your drive are marked, so you only choose what is new.

  3. Plug in and download

    Choose a folder or the USB drive the app detected, start the queue, and watch the MP3s land there one by one.

ck-you3 uses yt-dlp and ffmpeg. If either is missing, the app tells you before anything starts and shows how to install it.

First launch

ck-you3 is not signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate yet, so your system asks you to confirm once, the first time you open it.

On a Mac

  1. Open the .dmg, drag ck-you3 into Applications and open it. macOS says it cannot verify the app — close that message.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security (on macOS 12: System Preferences → Security & Privacy), scroll down and click Open Anyway next to ck-you3.
  3. Confirm when macOS asks again. From then on, ck-you3 opens like any other app.

On Windows

  1. Run the installer. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” screen appears, click More info.
  2. Click Run anyway. The installer finishes and ck-you3 starts like any other app.

Questions

Is ck-you3 free?

Yes. It is free to download and to use, with no account and no sign-in.

Does my history leave my computer?

No. The file you import, the list you browse and the MP3s you save all stay on your computer. There is no account and no server.

Where do I get my watch history?

From Google Takeout: select YouTube, choose JSON as the history format, and download the archive. The file to import is watch-history.json.

What else do I need to install?

yt-dlp and ffmpeg. The app checks for both before the queue starts and, if one is missing, says how to get it — for example with Homebrew on a Mac or winget on Windows.

Can it fill a USB stick for the car?

Yes. Plug the stick in, pick it as the destination, and start. Next time, songs already on the stick are skipped and only new ones are added.

Is downloading from YouTube allowed?

That is between you and YouTube. ck-you3 ships no content and bypasses no protection, and it asks you to acknowledge YouTube's Terms of Service before the first download.
